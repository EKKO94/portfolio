# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Adrian Carballo for Product Designer roles in B2B, AI and IoT. They skim fast, often on a phone between other tabs, and need to understand level, focus and proof of work within seconds before deciding to read a case study or get in touch.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents Adrian as a Product Designer who makes complex B2B, AI and IoT products clear and usable. It shows selected work with honest case studies (process, trade-offs, what is still unvalidated) and leads to one action: email him. Success is a recruiter opening a case study and then writing.

## Brand Personality

Editorial and direct, technical and precise, warm and human. Big confident type, generous white space and a single orange signal color; the work and the evidence do the talking. A real portrait and plain first-person copy keep it approachable.

## Anti-references

Generic "SaaS / AI template" looks: gradient text, glassmorphism, identical icon-and-heading card grids, hero-metric blocks, decorative motion. Nothing that feels generated. The Figma design is the source of truth for direction.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over decoration: screenshots, decisions and trade-offs carry the page; ornament does not.
2. One clear action: contact is always one tap away, with a single primary CTA per area.
3. Honest and specific: say what was done, what was learned and what is not yet validated.
4. Readable first: strong hierarchy and comfortable measure on every viewport, phone included.
5. Quiet motion: subtle reveals and hover feedback only, always optional under reduced motion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA (assumed; not specified by the owner). Keyboard-operable with visible focus, semantic landmarks, descriptive alt text, text contrast of at least 4.5:1, tap targets of at least 44px, and full support for prefers-reduced-motion. Copy is English.
