Story

Curious about how people think when they use technology.

I’m a computer engineer. I like to dig a little deeper into how people think and act when they interact with technology, so I can humanize the process and design an empathetic solution to the problem.

My engineering background, which includes front-end development, helps me talk with developers early about what is feasible. I’m adaptable and I love learning.

Recent work: Sorbot, an AI assistant inside the workflow and Vision, from forms to a node canvas.

How I work

Collaborative, empathetic and always learning.

  • I work with the whole team

    Product, engineering and stakeholders shape the problem together, and I bring them into the design early.

  • I start from how people think

    I try to understand how someone approaches a task before deciding what the interface should do.

  • I adapt and I learn

    I’m comfortable under pressure, and I pick up new domains quickly, from SEO to AI and computer vision.
